General Information

I am senior research scientist at Google Research. Previously, I was a post-doctoral scholar at the Institute for Advanced Study from September 2019 to August 2020, and at the Princeton TCS group from September 2020 to August 2021. I received my Ph.D. from University of California Berkeley where I was advised by Alistair Sinclair. I am currently mainly focusing on techniques for improving Large Language Models.
